# Privacy and compliance checklist

- Obtain counsel review for TCPA/A2P SMS, WhatsApp Business terms/templates, GDPR/UK GDPR, ePrivacy, state privacy laws, minors, and automated decisioning.
- Publish channel-specific notice and consent receipts; support STOP, START, HELP, quiet hours, suppression, export, deletion, and retention expiry.
- Minimize phone identifiers and message content, encrypt at rest and in transit, redact logs, and separate tenant keys.
- Document subprocessors, cross-border transfers, incident response, DSAR timelines, human escalation, and lawful-basis changes.

The prototype provides primitives and demo flows; it does not establish legal compliance.
